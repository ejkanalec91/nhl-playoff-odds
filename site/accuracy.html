How we tested

We replayed the 2023-24, 2024-25 and 2025-26 seasons. Before each game day the model was refit using only games already played, exactly as it runs live, then its predictions were compared with what happened.

The model's settings (how much last season counts, how fast ratings react, how blowouts are capped) were chosen using only 2023-24 and 2024-25. 2025-26 was held out as a test the model never saw while being tuned, so its numbers are the most honest estimate of live accuracy.

Brier score is the average of (prediction − outcome)². Log loss is similar but punishes confident misses much harder. For both, lower is better.
